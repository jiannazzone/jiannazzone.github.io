Welcome to adamOS

Adam Iannazzone is a self-taught programmer, photographer, and former high school teacher. In his spare time, he likes to code iOS Apps, build websites, and help local non-profits with their tech stack, such as websites, email, and Google Workspace.

An avid runner, you can find him hitting the trails in the Hudson Valley all times of year, although in the winter, he's more likely to spend the weekends snowboarding or bouldering at the local gym.

You can also find him on the airwaves as an amateur radio operator with the callsign K2CAT.
