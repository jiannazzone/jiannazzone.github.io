calcusplit.txt

CalcuSplit is an all-in-one solution for track coaches and event organizers.

With CalcuSplit, you can:
  • Track multiple runners, each with their own stopwatch.
  • Switch between races and workouts, allowing you to incorporate rest periods in between laps.
  • Export your results as HTML for easy emailing or CSV for your own detailed analysis.
  • Bulk-import your runners from a variety of spreadsheet sources.
  • No telemetry or tracking! No login required!

Get it on the iOS App Store!

calcurun.txt

CalcuRun is your personal running training and tracking companion. And it's totally free!

Just put in your most recent race data, and CalcuRun will use a science-based formula to predict how well you'll do in other events. It even provides training paces!

You can also view your historical trends; the app will normalize all of your races, regardless of distance to compare efforts and track your overall fitness.

lettercode.txt

Become a code-breaking master with LetterCode, a twist on the classic code-breaking formula.

A twist on the classic puzzle game, you have to guess the word before running out of chances. But don't take too long; the faster you solve, the more points you'll get!

LetterCode is free to play 5x per day, but a one-time purchase will unlock unlimited plays and higher difficulties. You can also see your stats, notable victories, and more!

The app also includes colorblind-friendly options, plus various play-style accessibility options.
